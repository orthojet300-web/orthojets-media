# ar04 — لماذا تنثني عظام الأطفال ولا تنكسر؟ (كسر الغصن الأخضر) — sources (PubMed)

| Claim (script / screen) | Source |
|---|---|
| Children's bone: lower stiffness (modulus), lower mineral (ash) content, deflects more and absorbs more energy before breaking; greenstick fracture surface typical of children's bone | Currey & Butler, J Bone Joint Surg Am 1975 (PMID 1158919) |
| Immature skeleton: thick periosteum, open physes (growth plates), powerful remodelling capacity; most paediatric fractures unite and realign without surgery | Bašković et al., Med Sci (Basel) 2026 (PMID 42646565, doi:10.3390/medsci14040432); Krieg et al., J Child Orthop 2026 (PMID 43047448, doi:10.1177/18632521261508965) |
| Greenstick = incomplete fracture with cortical disruption (one side breaks, other bends); buckle (torus) = incomplete compression fracture without cortical disruption; both common in children; fall on outstretched hand is the most common mechanism; radiography/ultrasound confirms; depending on angulation managed with immobilisation | Patel et al., Am Fam Physician 2021 (PMID 33719378) |
| Distal forearm (wrist) fractures in children: greenstick fracture of the radius = 48% of 4,316 fractures (Denmark, population-based) | Korup et al., Bone Joint Open 2022 (PMID 35658607, doi:10.1302/2633-1462.36.BJO-2022-0040.R1) |
| Buckle and non-displaced incomplete fractures: removable splint or bandage safe and effective (high-quality evidence) | Snelling et al., Injury 2024 (PMID 39321542, doi:10.1016/j.injury.2024.111897) |
| Torus fractures: offer of a bandage equivalent to rigid immobilisation (FORCE RCT, 965 children) | Perry et al., Health Technol Assess 2022 (PMID 35904496, doi:10.3310/BDNS6122) |
| Warning signs after wrist trauma in children: swelling of the distal radius, tenderness on palpation, visible deformity, painful supination predict fracture (lay: pain when pressing the bone, swelling, will not use the arm → see a doctor) | Slaar et al., Pediatr Radiol 2016 (PMID 26298555, doi:10.1007/s00247-015-3436-3) |

Note: in Korup 2022 the 48% "greenstick radius" category includes torus (buckle) fractures (Fig. 1 legend) — narration says "of this type" (both), screen says "greenstick or buckle".
