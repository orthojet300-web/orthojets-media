# ar02 — التواء أم كسر؟ (Ottawa ankle rules) — sources (PubMed)

| Claim (script / screen) | Source |
|---|---|
| Ankle joint: tibia, fibula, talus meet; two malleoli on either side; ligaments hold the bones; sprain = ligament stretched/torn with intact bone; fracture = broken bone | standard anatomy; Tiemstra, Am Fam Physician 2012 (PMID 22962897) — most sprains are inversion injuries to the lateral ankle ligaments |
| Most ankle injuries are not fractures; ≈15% are | Bachmann et al., BMJ 2003 (PMID 12595378, doi:10.1136/bmj.326.7386.417) — 15% prevalence used; Stiell et al., JAMA 1993 (PMID 8433468, doi:10.1001/jama.269.9.1127) — 121 malleolar + 49 midfoot fractures among 1032 adults |
| Doctors use a simple check: Ottawa ankle rules (Canada) | Stiell et al., Ann Emerg Med 1992 (PMID 1554175, doi:10.1016/s0196-0644(05)82656-3); Stiell et al., JAMA 1993 |
| 4 bony spots: posterior edge / tip of lateral and medial malleolus; base of 5th metatarsal; navicular | Tiemstra 2012; Stiell 1993 |
| Inability to walk 4 steps (immediately after injury and at the doctor) | Tiemstra 2012 ("inability to bear weight for four steps immediately after the injury and in the emergency department or physician's office") |
| Tenderness at a spot OR cannot walk → X-ray | Tiemstra 2012; Stiell 1993 |
| Rule negative → fracture chance very small (< 1.4%) | Bachmann 2003 (pooled negative LR 0.08; <1.4% at 15% prevalence; 27 studies, 15,581 patients) |
| Detects almost all fractures in adults and children (over 5): 98–100% | Bachmann 2003 (sensitivity almost 100%); Dowling et al., Acad Emerg Med 2009 (PMID 19187397, doi:10.1111/j.1553-2712.2008.00333.x) — children >5 y, pooled sensitivity 98.5% |
| Saves about one third of unnecessary X-rays | Stiell 1993 (potential reduction one third: 34% ankle, 30% foot); Bachmann 2003 (30–40%); Stiell, JAMA 1994 (PMID 8114236) — 28% fewer ankle X-rays in practice |
| See a doctor if you cannot walk or pain is not improving | safety-net advice (no treatment advice) |
