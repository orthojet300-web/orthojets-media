## What works / what doesn't
- **"Normal-looking X-ray, missed diagnosis" + a binary question is our best format.** Lisfranc: "Twisted midfoot, X-ray
  looks normal: sprain or Lisfranc injury?" → 82 saves and 28 shares on IG, 12 s watch. Saves = clinicians keep it as a
  reference (clear cut-offs: C1-MT2 gap, 2 mm, weight-bearing views).
- **Classification framed as one decision in a common adult/elderly patient works**: Garden "Hip fracture in an
  80-year-old: fix or replace?" 1,100 IG / 14 saves; earlier Gartland "cast or pins?" (178 TikTok, 13 likes).
- **Adult ED emergencies with a "tonight?" decision**: Kanavel 396 TikTok (best of week), 676 IG, 10 likes.
- **Weakest: niche oncology / score-named topics** — Mirels (IG 190, 3.2 s watch), and earlier MESS 7 and Salter-Harris.
  Ortho Numbers is the weakest series on both networks for the 3rd week.
- **Series (IG avg views, last 2 weeks):** Don't Miss far ahead (Lisfranc, Kanavel), Classified 2nd (Garden), Clinical Rules
  ~230, Ortho Numbers ~160. TikTok: all four ~340–400 this week, Don't Miss / Classified slightly ahead.
- Engagement: likes and saves are the reliable signals; A/B comment questions still give 0 real comments.
- Music: no consistent effect (calm_tiktok led last week, Mirels used it and was weakest this week; track04 was on Garden,
  Kanavel and fat embolism). Keep rotation. Visual theme: no visible effect.
