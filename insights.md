## What works / what doesn't
- Scheduled episodes all land at ~125–130 TikTok views: topic and series make no measurable difference yet.
- **Practical ED-decision questions do best across platforms** (Ottawa: "does this ankle need an X-ray?"). Niche paediatric
  criteria (Kocher) did worst on Instagram.
- **Instagram avg watch time is only 2–3 s on a ~62 s reel**: most viewers swipe away during the hook. The hook is the #1 problem.
- Music track (track01 / track02 / piano) and visual theme: no visible effect yet.

## Guidance for the next episodes
- Hook = a concrete clinical question or scenario the viewer faces in the ED/clinic ("Normal X-ray, sore snuffbox: send home?"),
  with the big number/answer appearing within the first second. Put the same question as the first caption line (search).
- Prefer topics that answer "what do I do now?" (red flags, rules, can't-miss) over pure classification lists; when doing a
  classification, frame it around the decision it changes.
- Make the engagement question a quick choice (A or B?) — open questions got no comments.
- Queue reordered 2026-10-25: Cauda equina red flags (dontmiss) moved up to follow Salter-Harris; series rotation kept.
