## What works / what doesn't
- **Best format (2 breakouts now): a common adult injury + a binary management question + a hard cut-off to save.**
  Weber "fibula broken at the joint line: fix or cast?" (new RCT data, 6 mm / 1 mm cut-offs) and Lisfranc
  "X-ray looks normal: sprain or Lisfranc?" (2 mm). Both: ~2% save rate, ~30 shares, 11–12 s watch.
- **Classified is now the strongest series on IG** (last 2 weeks avg ≈ 2,700: Weber 5,065, Schatzker 1,925, Garden 1,155),
  Don't Miss 2nd (≈ 1,700: Lisfranc, Klein, Kanavel), Clinical Rules ≈ 1,000 (Amsterdam 1,655), Ortho Numbers weakest
  (Mirels 229; no Numbers episode this week; knee ABI posts Nov 23).
- "Missed on the X-ray" works in children too: Klein's line (SCFE, "knee X-ray normal") 2,064 / 34 saves.
- Weakest this week: posterior shoulder (418 IG, 88 TikTok after 3 days — far behind every other episode at the same age).
  A rarer injury, and the "seizure" opener is less common than a fall. Judge again next week.
- TikTok does not react to topic: every episode lands at 340–400. Instagram decides which episode breaks out.
- Likes, saves and shares are the useful signals; A/B comment questions still give 0 real comments.
- Music: no effect (track01 had Weber and Klein, but also the flat C-spine; track05 had Lisfranc and the weak posterior
  shoulder). Keep rotation. Visual theme: no visible effect.

## Guidance for the next episodes
- Line 1 (= first caption line + TikTok title + hook card): common patient in one line + a binary decision,
  "...: fix or cast?" / "...: X-ray or not?" / "X-ray looks normal: ...?". Name the classification or sign after that.
- Prefer the most common ED/fracture-clinic injuries and decisions; lead with recent RCT or large-cohort numbers when available.
- 2–3 hard cut-offs drawn on the image (mm, degrees, %), then "Save it & share it with a colleague".
- First 1–2 s: movement + the key image, no title card.
- Ortho Numbers: choose bedside ED numbers tied to a decision (ABI 0.9, synovial WBC, 48 h), not scores from niche fields.
